# Minixx's Clippy

Instant replay for Geometry Dash on iOS, with a paperclip that has opinions.

Clippy keeps the **last seconds of your game** (video + game audio) in a small rolling buffer. When something worth keeping happens, he saves it as an `.mp4`:

- you **die past a percent you choose** (80% by default)
- you hit a **new best**
- you **beat the level**
- or you press **Clip** (pause menu, main menu or the Studio)

Open the **Studio** from the main menu or the pause menu to browse, share (Save Video, Messages, Files...) and delete your clips, and to tweak every option.

## Good to know

- iOS asks for **screen-recording permission every time the game starts**. That is an iOS rule for in-app capture.
- Nothing leaves your phone. The microphone is never used.
- The buffer lives in memory (about 45 MB for 30 s at the default quality).
- Clips are saved in the mod's save folder. Use the **Share** button to put them in Photos.
- Clippy's reactions are hidden while you play by default, so he never ends up in your next clip. You still get a vibration.
